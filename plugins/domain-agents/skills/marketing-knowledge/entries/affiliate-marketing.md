# Affiliate marketing

Source: startup-business-guide Seite 223. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/SU-affiliate-marketing.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

Affiliate marketing is a performance-based marketing model where businesses partner with affiliates (individuals or other businesses) to promote their products or services in exchange for a commission. It is a type of revenue sharing between businesses that have a product or service to sell and third-party marketers who promote it to their own audience. The process involves the merchant, the affiliate, and the customer.

- Merchant provides the product/service and tracks sales made through the affiliate link
- Affiliate promotes to their own audience (websites, social media, email, SEM) and receives a commission per sale via a unique affiliate link
- Customer purchases the product or service through the affiliate's link
- Program types: pay-per-click (PPC, paid per click), pay-per-sale (PPS, commission per sale), pay-per-lead (PPL, paid per lead/inquiry)
- Benefits: cost-effective wider reach and more sales for the merchant; commission opportunity for the affiliate
- Challenges: finding and managing the right affiliates (merchant); standing out and generating enough sales (affiliate)
