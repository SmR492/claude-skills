# Lifetime value (LTV)

The lifetime value (LTV) of a customer refers to the total amount of revenue a customer is expected to generate for a company during their lifetime as a customer. It is calculated from average purchase value, number of purchases per year, and average customer lifespan. LTV helps companies understand the long-term value of acquiring and retaining customers and make informed decisions about acquisition spend and retention investment, as well as identify the most valuable customer types.

- LTV = Average Purchase Value x Number of Purchases per Year x Average Customer Lifespan
- Example: $100 x 2 x 5 = $1,000
- Informs how much to spend on customer acquisition and retention
- Helps identify which customer types are most valuable to the business
