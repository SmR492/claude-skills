# Word-of-mouth marketing (WOMM)

Word-of-mouth marketing (WOMM) is a marketing strategy that aims to increase brand awareness, customer engagement, and sales by leveraging positive recommendations and referrals from satisfied customers. It involves encouraging customers to share their positive experiences in person or through online channels such as social media, review sites, and blogs. It is based on the premise that people trust the opinions of friends and family more than traditional advertising.

- Provide exceptional customer service and high-quality products/services that exceed expectations to delight customers
- Incentivize customers to share experiences (discounts or rewards for referrals, contests or giveaways)
- Leverage online review sites and social media platforms to amplify positive word-of-mouth
- Monitor and respond to customer reviews and comments to engage customers and build a community of brand advocates
