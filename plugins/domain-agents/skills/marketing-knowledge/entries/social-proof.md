# Social proof

Social proof is a psychological phenomenon that occurs when people make decisions based on the actions and opinions of others. People are more likely to believe and follow the actions of others, especially those in their social group or perceived as having authority or expertise. In marketing and advertising it is used to influence consumer behavior and increase sales by creating a sense of trust and credibility.

- Forms: customer reviews, testimonials, social media likes and shares, celebrity endorsements, expert recommendations
- Positive reviews make customers more likely to view a product favorably and consider purchasing
- Can lead to increased brand awareness, customer loyalty, and higher sales
- Negative effects: negative reviews/opinions reduce willingness to try; can lead to groupthink or herd mentality
