# Content marketing

Content marketing is a marketing strategy that focuses on creating and distributing valuable, relevant, and consistent content to attract and retain a clearly defined audience and ultimately drive profitable customer action. Businesses use it to build relationships with potential customers and establish their authority and expertise in an industry. Content is created to inform, educate, or entertain rather than directly promote a product.

- Forms: blog posts, videos, podcasts, social media updates, infographics, e-books, webinars, and more
- Key principles: understand the target audience, create high-quality content that resonates, and distribute through various channels
- Measured through metrics such as engagement, conversions, and ROI
- Attracts customers actively seeking information and establishes the business as a trustworthy authority
- Improves search engine rankings and drives more organic traffic, as helpful content is more likely to be shared and linked to
