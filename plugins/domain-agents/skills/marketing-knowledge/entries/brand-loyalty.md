# Brand loyalty

Source: startup-business-guide Seite 241. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/SU-brand-loyalty.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

Brand loyalty refers to the tendency of customers to consistently purchase products or services from a specific brand or company over an extended period. It is a crucial component of a company's success as it ensures long-term sales and revenue streams. It is established when customers develop an emotional connection to a brand, leading them to choose it over competitors.

- Influenced by the quality and consistency of products/services, the brand's reputation and image, and the customer experience
- Fostered by a strong brand identity, exceptional customer service, and consistently high-quality delivery
- Loyalty programs reward repeat business and build stronger connections with customers
- Loyal customers recommend the brand (word-of-mouth) and reduce the impact of competition, being less likely to switch
