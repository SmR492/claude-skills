# Brand positioning

Brand positioning is the process of defining and communicating a brand's unique value proposition and competitive advantage to its target audience. The goal is to identify a unique position in the market that is relevant and meaningful to the target audience and sustainable over time. It is typically communicated through channels such as advertising, public relations, social media, content marketing, and experiential marketing.

- Target audience: deep understanding of needs, desires, values, attitudes, and behaviors
- Competitive analysis: analyze competitors' strengths and weaknesses to find a unique position
- Unique value proposition: identify clear, compelling benefits different from competitors
- Brand promise: a clear, consistent promise (quality, reliability, innovation, service) across all channels
