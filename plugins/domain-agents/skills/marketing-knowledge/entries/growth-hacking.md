# Growth hacking

Growth hacking refers to a set of marketing strategies and tactics aimed at achieving rapid and sustained growth for a company or product. It involves using data-driven experimentation, creativity, and unconventional methods to rapidly test and iterate on different marketing approaches.

- Key tenets: focus on metrics (acquisition, activation, retention, referral); rapid experimentation; think outside the box; leverage platforms; use cross-functional approaches
- Example tactics: A/B testing, content marketing, search engine optimization (SEO), viral marketing, referral programs
