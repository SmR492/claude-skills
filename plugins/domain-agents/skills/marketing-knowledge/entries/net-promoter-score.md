# Net Promoter Score (NPS)

Net Promoter Score (NPS) is a metric used to measure customer loyalty and satisfaction by asking how likely a customer is to recommend the product/service to a friend or colleague on a scale of 0-10. It is calculated by subtracting the percentage of detractors from the percentage of promoters, giving a score from -100 to +100, where higher indicates greater satisfaction. NPS should be supplemented with other metrics and qualitative feedback rather than relied on as the sole measure.

- Detractors (0-6): dissatisfied, unlikely to recommend, can harm the brand via negative word-of-mouth
- Passives (7-8): satisfied but not enthusiastic, more likely to switch to a competitor
- Promoters (9-10): highly satisfied, likely to recommend and make repeat purchases
- NPS = % Promoters − % Detractors, ranging from -100 to +100
- Useful for tracking loyalty over time and benchmarking, but has limitations
