# Sales funnel

A sales funnel, also known as a purchase funnel, is a visual representation of the customer journey from initial contact with a business or product to the final purchase. Its purpose is to understand and optimize the customer journey.

- Awareness: potential customers become aware of the product/service via advertising, social media, or other channels
- Interest: customers show interest, visiting the website, reading blog posts, or engaging on social media
- Decision: customers consider purchasing (request a quote, sign up for a free trial, contact sales)
- Action: customers become paying customers (purchase, subscribe, or become a client)
