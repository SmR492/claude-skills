# Voice of the Customer (VoC)

Voice of the Customer (VoC) refers to the process of capturing customer feedback, opinions, preferences, and needs regarding a product or service. Its goal is to capture and analyze customer feedback through channels such as surveys, focus groups, customer support interactions, and social media, so organizations can better understand customers and make informed decisions to meet their needs.

- Channels: surveys, focus groups, customer support interactions, social media, other feedback mechanisms
- Improved customer satisfaction by meeting understood needs
- Increased customer loyalty and retention by acting on feedback
- Enhanced product development driven by customer feedback
- Better decision-making about where to invest resources and prioritize efforts
