# Guerilla marketing

Guerilla marketing is an advertising strategy that involves creative, unconventional, and low-cost tactics to promote a product, service, or brand. The term implies tactics that are unexpected, unconventional, and often ambush-style in nature. Unlike traditional paid advertising, it aims to generate buzz and a memorable impression using non-traditional means, and is often associated with smaller businesses and startups with limited budgets.

- Stunts or events: a memorable event or spectacle to draw attention to a brand or product
- Ambush marketing: associating a brand with a popular event or trend without officially sponsoring it
- Street art or graffiti: artistic, provocative displays in public spaces
- Viral marketing: online content designed to be shared widely and generate buzz
- Product sampling: free samples to generate interest and word-of-mouth
- Can be highly effective (buzz, memorable impression, word-of-mouth) but risky if seen as intrusive or offensive when not executed carefully
