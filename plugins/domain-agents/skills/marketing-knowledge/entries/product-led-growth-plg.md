# Product Led Growth (PLG)

Product Led Growth (PLG) is a business strategy that puts the product at the center of the customer acquisition and retention process. Instead of relying on traditional marketing and sales, PLG aims to create a product so compelling that it "sells itself" and drives growth through user adoption, satisfaction, and word-of-mouth referrals. It is based on the idea that experiencing a product's value upfront makes customers more likely to become loyal users and advocates.

- Focus on the user: prioritize UX and solve real problems
- Make it easy to start: self-serve and easy to use without extensive training
- Use data to optimize the product: collect and analyze data to improve UX
- Leverage network effects: value increases as more users adopt
- Align the organization around the product rather than traditional departments
- Successful examples: Slack, Zoom, Dropbox
