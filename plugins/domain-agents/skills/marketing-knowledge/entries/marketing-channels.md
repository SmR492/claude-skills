# Marketing channels

Marketing channels are the different ways a company can reach its target audience to promote and sell its products or services. The choice of channels depends on factors such as the target audience, the product or service, the budget, and the marketing objectives. Companies should choose the most effective channels for their business.

- Digital channels: social media, email marketing, search engine marketing (SEM), search engine optimization (SEO), mobile marketing, online advertising
- Physical channels: retail stores, direct mail, outdoor advertising, billboards
- Broadcast media: television and radio advertisements
- Print media: newspapers, magazines, brochures, flyers
- Events: trade shows, conferences, product launches, other live events
- Referral marketing: word-of-mouth, customer reviews, recommendations
