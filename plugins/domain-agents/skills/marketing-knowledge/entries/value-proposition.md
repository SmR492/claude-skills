# Value proposition

A value proposition is a statement describing the unique value a company or product offers to its customers, outlining benefits and solutions and what sets it apart from competitors. It should be clear, concise, and relevant, and focus on benefits and measurable outcomes rather than just features.

- Should be clear, concise, and relevant to the target audience
- Answers why a customer should choose this company or product over competitors
- Requires understanding the target audience's needs, pain points, and challenges
- Analyze competitors' value propositions to identify gaps in the market to fill
- Focus on benefits and solutions with specific, measurable outcomes, not just features
