# Go-to-market strategy (GTM strategy)

A go-to-market (GTM) strategy is a comprehensive plan that outlines how a business will bring a new product or service to market and achieve commercial success. It defines the target market, customer segments, marketing channels, pricing, sales and distribution strategy, and competitive positioning. It is an essential part of the product launch process, often developed in parallel with product development, aiming to create a roadmap for revenue growth, customer acquisition, and market share.

- Identify target market and customer segments via market research (size, competition, demand)
- Determine best marketing channels (advertising, social media, SEO, events)
- Set optimal pricing strategy to maximize revenue and profitability
- Define sales and distribution strategy (direct sales, partnerships, e-commerce)
- Define competitive positioning and a unique value proposition
