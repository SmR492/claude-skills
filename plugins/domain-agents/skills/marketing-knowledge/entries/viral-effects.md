# Viral effects

Ref: `SU:viral-effects` · Category: principle · Entry version: 1
Source: startup-business-guide Seite 95. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/SU-viral-effects.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

The phenomenon where a product or service spreads rapidly through word-of-mouth or social sharing, similar to a virus. Customers become advocates and share positive experiences, leading to a self-sustaining cycle of growth and adoption. A powerful marketing tool, especially online, but it can also work in reverse with negative word-of-mouth.

- Social media platforms (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram) let customers share experiences and reach broad audiences
- Amplified by innovative/unique products, strong brand presence, and leveraging influencers
- Building a strong community creates a sense of belonging and drives further adoption
- Reverse risk: negative experiences spread just as quickly, damaging reputation — deliver high-quality products and excellent service
