# UX knowledge pack

- `entries/INDEX.md` lists the 118 UX entries; load the ones a task names.
- Entries are bulk extractions from a design guide and marked unverified. WCAG version and conformance level are set per project by its owner; the entries do not decide them. Legal accessibility duties are a legal question with a primary-source check.
- The dormant role texts `roles-dormant/ux-designer.md` and `roles-dormant/ux-reviewer.md` describe how a designer and a reviewer work with this pack; they become agents at the third logged UX run.
