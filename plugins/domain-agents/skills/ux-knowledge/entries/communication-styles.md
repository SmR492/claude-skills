# Communication styles

Communication styles are the patterns and preferences individuals or cultures have in conveying and interpreting messages. Three common dimensions are direct or indirect, high-context or low-context, and individualistic or collectivist. Understanding these styles helps adapt one's approach to different cultural contexts, promoting effective communication, building rapport, and avoiding misunderstandings.

- Direct vs Indirect: explicit/straightforward expression versus reliance on context, subtlety, hints, and nonverbal cues
- High-Context vs Low-Context: contextual/nonverbal/shared assumptions versus explicit verbal communication
- Individualistic vs Collectivist: personal goals/independence/self-expression versus group harmony/cooperation/social relationships
