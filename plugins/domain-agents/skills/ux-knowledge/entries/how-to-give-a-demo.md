# How to give a demo

Giving a demo involves showcasing a product, service, or concept to an audience, whether in-person, through a virtual presentation, or a combination. The excerpt outlines steps from knowing your audience to following up.

- Know Your Audience: determine needs, interests, goals; tailor to pain points
- Set Objectives: structure the demo to generate interest, educate, or make a sale
- Plan and Prepare: organize content, messages, visuals; create a storyboard/outline; practice
- Be Engaging: attention-grabbing introduction, then purpose, unique aspects, key features and benefits
- Be Dynamic: vary tone, pace, gestures; consider interactivity like quizzes, polls, hands-on exercises
- Use Visuals: slides, videos, walkthroughs, demonstrations
- Tell Stories: real-life examples, case studies, testimonials
- Address Concerns: invite questions, be prepared to address concerns and clarify
- End with a Call to Action: summarize key points, reinforce benefits, state desired actions
- Follow-Up: encourage feedback and send personalized messages to nurture the relationship
