# Workflow Analysis

Workflow Analysis, also known as Process Analysis, is a systematic examination and evaluation of the sequence of activities, tasks, and information flow within a system, organization, or process. It aims to understand how work happens, identify inefficiencies and opportunities, clarify roles and responsibilities, and optimize the workflow.

- Workflow Mapping: represent the process, context, and relationships via flowcharts, diagrams, or process maps
- Task Analysis: examine each activity's purpose, inputs, outputs, and required resources
- Time Analysis: measure time per activity to find time-consuming steps and bottlenecks
- Data Flow Analysis: analyze how data flows to ensure smooth communication
- Roles and Responsibilities: examine roles, handoffs, and areas of confusion or duplication
- Decisions: analyze the decision-making process, criteria, rules, and dependencies
- Controls: examine feedback mechanisms (customer input, employee suggestions, performance metrics) to drive improvements
