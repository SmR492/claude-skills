# Microcopy

Ref: `UX:microcopy` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: ui-ux-design-guide Seite 122. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/UX-microcopy.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

Microcopy refers to the small, concise blocks of text used throughout digital interfaces to guide and assist users in their interactions. It includes labels, instructions, error messages, tooltips, button texts, and other short snippets that provide clarity, direction, and feedback to users.

- Clarity: clear, concise, and easy to understand, without ambiguity or jargon
- Contextual Relevance: aligns with the user's current situation and provides guidance applicable to immediate needs
- User-Focused Language: user-centric, matching the audience's preferences, knowledge level, and language proficiency
- Tone and Voice: reflects the brand's tone and voice for a consistent experience
- Help and Guidance: provides tips and suggestions and preemptively answers questions or addresses common issues
- Error Handling: concise, specific error messages with actionable suggestions to guide recovery
