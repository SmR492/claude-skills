A user journey, also known as a customer journey, is a tool used in product design and development to map out the path a user takes to complete a specific task or achieve a goal. It is a visual representation of the steps a user goes through, from initial awareness to final outcome. User journeys are often created alongside personas and help identify pain points and areas for improvement.

- A series of steps from initial trigger to final outcome
- Each step is accompanied by the user's actions, thoughts, emotions, and any pain points
- Created through user research: interviews, surveys, and other data collection
- Help ensure the product is user-focused and meets the needs of its intended audience
