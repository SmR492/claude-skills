# Subject Matter Expert (SME)

A Subject Matter Expert (SME) is an individual who possesses specialized knowledge, expertise, and experience in a specific subject area or field. SMEs provide subject-specific insights, guidance, and support. Their expertise is typically acquired through years of education, work in a specific industry, or conducting research.

- Expertise and Knowledge: advise colleagues/stakeholders, clarify complex concepts, shape priorities, accelerate decision-making
- Knowledge Transfer: design/deliver training, documentation, presentations, or research articles
- Collaboration Guidance: advise cross-functional teams, project managers, and stakeholders
- Continuous Learning: stay up-to-date with advancements, trends, research, technologies, and regulations
