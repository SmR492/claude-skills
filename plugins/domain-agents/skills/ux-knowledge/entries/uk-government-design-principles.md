# UK Government Design Principles

The UK Government Design Principles are a set of principles guiding the design of government services. They emphasize starting with user needs, doing less, and being data-driven, inclusive, and open. They stress building services rather than websites and being consistent, not uniform.

- Start with user needs
- Do less
- Design with data
- Do the hard work to make it simple
- Iterate
- This is for everyone
- Understand context
- Build services, not websites
- Be consistent, not uniform
- Make things open
