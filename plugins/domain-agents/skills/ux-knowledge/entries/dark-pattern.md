# Dark pattern

Ref: `UX:dark-pattern` · Category: technique · Entry version: 1
Source: ui-ux-design-guide Seite 111. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/UX-dark-pattern.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

A dark pattern in UI/UX design refers to deceptive techniques used to influence user behavior. These patterns exploit cognitive biases and psychological principles to trick or mislead users. Dark patterns are unethical and harm the user experience.

- Misdirection: design intentionally draws attention away from certain options or information, leading to unintended choices
- Urgency: false or exaggerated time pressure pushes quick decisions without adequate consideration
- Forced Continuity: users enrolled in a free trial/subscription without explicit consent, and it is hard to exit
- Roach Motel: easy to get into a situation, but challenging or impossible to leave
- Hidden Costs: extra fees, charges, or subscriptions hidden or obscured via fine print or asterisk disclaimers
- Sneak into Basket: items or services added to the cart without consent, often during checkout
- Friend Spam: exploits users' personal network to share/invite friends without explicit consent
