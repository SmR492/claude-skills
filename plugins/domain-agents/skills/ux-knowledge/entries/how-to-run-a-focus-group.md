# How to run a focus group

Running a focus group involves gathering a small group of individuals with similar characteristics or experiences to gather insights, opinions, and feedback on a specific topic or product. The excerpt lists steps from defining purpose through to reporting findings.

- Define the Purpose: clarify objectives and research questions/areas of interest
- Recruit Participants: choose participant characteristics aligned with objectives; communicate purpose, time commitment, incentives
- Plan Logistics: date, time, location, comfortable quiet environment, necessary equipment
- Develop a Guide: structured discussion guide with key topics, questions, activities and logical flow
- Facilitate Focus: introduce yourself, explain purpose and guidelines, encourage open sharing, actively listen
- Manage Group Dynamics: foster interaction, ensure all contribute, manage dominant or overly quiet participants
- Report Findings: analyze results for key findings, quotes, anecdotes; share a report with stakeholders
