# Mobile-first design

Mobile-first design is a design philosophy in which the design process starts with a design specifically for mobile devices, then scales it up to larger screens such as tablets and desktops. It focuses on an optimal user experience on smaller screens with limited space, processing power, and bandwidth, forcing designers to prioritize content and features.

- Design for the smallest screen (typically a smartphone) first, then work up to larger screens
- Encourages responsive design and progressive enhancement for a consistent experience across all devices
- Important because mobile is the primary device for internet access: more than 50% of internet traffic comes from mobile devices and is increasing
- SEO benefits: Google ranks mobile-friendly sites higher and uses mobile-first indexing (mobile version as primary for indexing/ranking)
