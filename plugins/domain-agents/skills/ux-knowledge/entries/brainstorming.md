# Brainstorming

Brainstorming is a creative problem-solving technique used to generate a large number of ideas in a short period of time. The goal is to promote a free-flowing, non-linear environment that allows exploration of diverse perspectives related to a topic or problem. Participants suspend judgment during idea generation, and evaluate the ideas afterward.

- Quantity over Quality: generate many ideas without immediately evaluating feasibility
- Non-judgmental Atmosphere: refrain from criticizing ideas during the phase
- Free Thinking: encourage unconventional, imaginative, even wild ideas
- Build on Ideas: build on or combine others' ideas to promote collaboration
- Participation: give everyone a chance to contribute (round-robin, equal airtime)
- Timebox: set a time limit to maintain focus and energy
- Capture: document ideas in a visible, accessible format (whiteboard, flipchart, web app)
