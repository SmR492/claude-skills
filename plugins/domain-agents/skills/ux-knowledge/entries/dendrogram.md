A dendrogram is a graphical representation used in fields such as biology, data science, and taxonomy to illustrate the arrangement of objects based on their similarity or dissimilarity. It is commonly used in hierarchical clustering analysis, where each object is a leaf node connected into a tree-like structure and the length of branches indicates the degree of similarity. It helps understand how items relate and where to cut the tree to form clusters at different levels of granularity.

- Each object or data point is represented as a leaf node connected into a tree structure
- Branch length indicates degree of similarity or dissimilarity between objects
- More similar objects group at lower levels; less similar ones are farther apart
- Cutting the tree at different points forms clusters at different levels of granularity
