# Graceful degradation

Ref: `UX:graceful-degradation` · Category: principle · Entry version: 1
Source: ui-ux-design-guide Seite 130. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/UX-graceful-degradation.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

Graceful degradation, in UI/UX design, is a design approach that ensures a website or application still functions and remains usable even when certain features or technologies are not supported or available in the user's environment. It is the complement of progressive enhancement.

- User Experience: provide a positive experience for all users regardless of device, browser, or technological limitations, keeping core functionality accessible
- Design Prioritization: identify and prioritize essential features/content that work across different platforms
- Feature Detection: detect the capabilities of the user's device/browser and adapt the experience accordingly
- Responsive Design: layout and content adapt to the user's screen size and device capabilities
- Robust Error Handling: provide clear, informative error messages and alternative paths when a capability is unavailable
