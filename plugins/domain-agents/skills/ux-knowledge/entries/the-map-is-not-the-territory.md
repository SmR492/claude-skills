# The map is not the territory

"The map is not the territory" is a phrase coined by Alfred Korzybski to illustrate a concept in semantics and perception: a subjective understanding or representation of reality (the map) is not the same as the actual reality or experience itself (the territory). It encourages approaching situations with openness, curiosity, and willingness to consider multiple perspectives, recognizing the limitations and biases in our mental maps.

- Subjective Interpretation: each person builds their own mental map from experiences and beliefs
- Abstraction and Simplification: maps condense complex information into a simplified form
- Limitations and Incompleteness: maps can never fully capture the complexity of the territory
- Perception and Bias: biases and preconceptions distort our understanding and communication
