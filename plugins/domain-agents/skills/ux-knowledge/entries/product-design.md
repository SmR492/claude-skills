# Product design

Product design is the process of creating new products or improving existing ones to meet the needs of consumers. It involves designing, prototyping, testing, and iterating until a final product is developed that meets users' needs, solves their problems, and provides a delightful user experience. Throughout the process the design team collaborates with stakeholders to ensure the product aligns with business goals and the market.

- Begins with understanding user needs, pain points, and behaviors through research and analysis
- Uses research to create personas, user stories, and use cases
- Creates sketches, wireframes, and mockups to explore options and present to stakeholders
- Builds high-fidelity prototypes mimicking look, feel, and functionality once a direction is approved
- Tests with real users to gather usability, desirability, and functionality feedback, then iterates
- Collaborates with product managers, engineers, marketing, and sales teams
