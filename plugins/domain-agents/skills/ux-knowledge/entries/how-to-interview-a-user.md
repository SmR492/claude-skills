# How to interview a user

Interviewing users is a valuable method for gathering insights, understanding their needs, and gaining feedback. The excerpt outlines steps from defining objectives to concluding the interview.

- Define: determine objectives, create a structured interview guide, arrange logistics and materials
- Establish Rapport: introduce yourself, explain purpose, create a friendly comfortable environment
- Ask Open-Ended Questions: use open-ended non-biased questions for detailed responses
- Listen Actively: attend to verbal and non-verbal cues like tone, facial expressions, body language
- Dig Deeper: probe with follow-up questions to gain deeper insights
- Empathize and Seek Context: explore background, goals, challenges, and specific situations
- Respect Silence and Pauses: give users time to think; avoid rushing or interrupting
- Conclusion: summarize insights, confirm the user's perspective, thank them, offer follow-up
