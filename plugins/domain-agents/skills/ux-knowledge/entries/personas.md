Personas are a tool used in product design and development to create a representation of the typical user of a product. A user persona is a fictional character representing a group of users who share similar goals, needs, motivations, and behaviors, based on research and analysis of real users. The goal is to provide a clear understanding of users and their needs to inform design and development decisions.

- A typical persona includes a name, photo, demographic information, job title, and brief description of goals/motivations
- May add behavior patterns, pain points, and preferences
- Created through user research: interviews, surveys, and other data collection
- Data is analyzed to identify patterns and trends used to build the persona
