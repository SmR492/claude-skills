# North Star

In business terminology, the North Star is a singular, overarching goal or objective that guides a company's decision-making and strategy, keeping it focused on what is most important and driving it toward its long-term vision. It is often used in agile and lean startup methodologies to stay focused, avoid distractions, and make effective decisions under uncertainty. For some companies it is expressed as a key metric (or set of metrics) tied to the company's mission and vision.

- Guiding principle to align efforts, stay motivated, and measure progress
- Can be expressed as a key metric such as customer satisfaction, revenue growth, or market share
- Example: Airbnb's North Star metric is 'nights booked'
