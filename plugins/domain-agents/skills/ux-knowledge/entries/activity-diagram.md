An activity diagram is a type of behavioral diagram in software engineering that describes the flow of activities or actions within a system or process. It is a graphical representation of the steps in a workflow or business process and can model complex systems or business processes.

- Activities: tasks/actions, shown as rounded-corner rectangles (e.g. 'Withdraw Money')
- Transitions: connections showing flow of control, shown as labeled arrows (e.g. 'Verify Account')
- Decisions: points where flow splits based on a condition, shown as a diamond (e.g. 'Has Sufficient Balance?')
- Swimlanes: indicate participation of different actors or departments, shown as labeled rectangles
