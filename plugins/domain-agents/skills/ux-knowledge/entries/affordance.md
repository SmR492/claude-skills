# Affordance

Affordance is a concept in design and psychology referring to the perceived and intuitive relationship between an object or element and its potential actions or uses, describing qualities that suggest how it can or should be interacted with. Affordances give users cues about how to interact with a physical object, digital interface, or designed artifact, but can be misleading or ambiguous, leading to usability issues. Clear affordances enhance usability and learnability, bridging the gap between a user's goals and the actions needed to achieve them.

- Perceptible Affordances: physical or visual characteristics that convey use (e.g. a round doorknob affords grasping and turning; a flat surface affords pushing; a raised button affords pressing)
- Hidden or Implied Affordances: cues inferred from past experience or cultural conventions (e.g. a flat touchscreen icon implies tapping/swiping; underlined text implies a hyperlink)
