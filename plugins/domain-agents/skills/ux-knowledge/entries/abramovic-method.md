The Abramović Method is a set of exercises and techniques developed by performance artist Marina Abramović. It is designed to enhance awareness, presence, and focus for both performers and audience members.

- Presence: being fully present in the moment and letting go of distractions
- Stillness: cultivating inner stillness and silence for a deeper connection
- Concentration: exercises that develop heightened concentration and awareness
- Durational Performance: exploring extended periods of concentration and presence
- Mindfulness and Sensory Awareness: practices enhancing awareness of body, sensations, and environment
- Ritual and Ceremony: elements that create intention, reverence, and heightened focus
