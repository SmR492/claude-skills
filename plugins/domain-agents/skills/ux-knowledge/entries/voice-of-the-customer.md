# Voice of the Customer (VoC)

Voice of the Customer (VoC) refers to the process of capturing customer feedback, opinions, preferences, and needs regarding a particular product or service. It is a way for organizations to better understand their customers and make informed decisions. The goal is to capture and analyze feedback through channels such as surveys, focus groups, customer support interactions, and social media.

- Improved customer satisfaction by understanding what customers want and need
- Increased customer loyalty by acting on feedback and building relationships
- Enhanced product development driven by customer feedback
- Better decision-making about where to invest resources and prioritize efforts
