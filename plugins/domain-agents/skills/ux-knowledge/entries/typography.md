Typography is the art and technique of arranging and designing typefaces (fonts) to make written language readable and visually appealing. It involves the selection, arrangement, and styling of typefaces, plus considerations of spacing, line length, and layout, and plays a crucial role in graphic design, web design, branding, and visual communication.

- Typefaces: fonts range from serif to sans-serif, script, and display fonts
- Readability: font size, line spacing (leading), letter spacing (tracking/kerning), and text-background contrast ensure readability
- Emotional Impact: typefaces can evoke elegance, playfulness, seriousness, or informality and set the tone
- Hierarchy: different sizes, weights, and styles guide reader attention across headings, subheadings, and body text
- Alignment: left, right, centered, or justified affects visual flow and readability
- Consistency: consistent typography creates a cohesive visual identity and reinforces recognition
