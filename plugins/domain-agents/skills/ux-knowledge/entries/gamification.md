# Gamification

Source: ui-ux-design-guide Seite 34. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/UX-gamification.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

Gamification is the process of incorporating game-like elements and mechanics into non-game environments to make them more engaging and enjoyable. It is used in contexts from education and training to marketing and advertising to encourage participation and increase motivation and engagement. It leverages the motivational power of games through elements such as points, badges, leaderboards, challenges, and rewards.

- Elements include points, badges, leaderboards, challenges, and rewards
- Used to achieve objectives (motivate employees, encourage purchases, aid learning)
- Process: analyze audience, identify target behaviors, design and incorporate mechanics, launch and monitor effectiveness
- Game elements should be well-designed and not distract from the underlying activity
