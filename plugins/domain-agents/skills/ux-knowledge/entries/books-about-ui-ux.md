# Books about UI/UX

Source: ui-ux-design-guide Seite 155. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/UX-books-about-ui-ux.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

Eine kuratierte Liste populaerer und stark empfohlener Buecher im Feld UI/UX, jeweils mit kurzer Beschreibung des Schwerpunkts. Die Liste umfasst Grundlagenwerke zu Design, Usability, Interface-Patterns, Interaktionsdesign und Informationsarchitektur.

- The Design of Everyday Things - Don Norman (Psychologie des human-centered Designs)
- Designing Interfaces - Jenifer Tidwell (Interface-Design-Patterns)
- Universal Principles of Design - Lidwell, Holden, Butler (125 Design-Prinzipien)
- The Elements of User Experience - Jesse James Garrett (Strategy, Scope, Structure, Surface)
- Interaction Design: Beyond Human-Computer Interaction - Helen Sharp, Yvonne Rogers, Jenny Preece
- Designing for Interaction - Dan Saffer
- Information Architecture: For the Web and Beyond - Louis Rosenfeld & Peter Morville
