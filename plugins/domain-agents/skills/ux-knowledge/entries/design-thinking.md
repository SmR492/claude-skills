# Design thinking

Design thinking is a problem-solving approach that emphasizes empathizing with the end-user to understand problems, ideate potential solutions, and iterate until a solution is achieved. It can apply to a wide range of areas, from product design to user experience design to organizational design. It is a flexible and iterative process that keeps designers focused on the user's needs.

- Empathize: understand needs, desires, and pain points via research, interviews, observation
- Define: reframe and define the problem based on empathize-stage insights
- Ideate: generate solutions via brainstorming, sketching, and creative techniques
- Prototype: create tangible representations (physical prototypes, digital mockups) to test and refine
- Test: test prototypes with users to gain feedback and iterate until a final solution
