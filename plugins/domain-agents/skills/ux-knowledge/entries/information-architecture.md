# Information architecture (IA)

Information architecture (IA) is the practice of organizing, structuring, and labeling information to support effective navigation, findability, and understanding within a system such as a website, application, or database. A well-designed IA helps improve user efficiency and enhances user satisfaction. Information architects work closely with designers, developers, content creators, and stakeholders.

- Structure: organize information meaningfully; categories, annotations, tags, and relationships reflecting users' mental models
- Navigation Design: menus, navigation bars, search, and interactive elements to move through the information space
- Labeling and Metadata: clear descriptive labels that accurately represent content and actions
- Search Functionality: search interfaces, algorithms, result optimization, and search metrics
- User-Centered Design: understand user needs via research (interviews, testing, focus groups, surveys)
- Content Strategy: collaborate with content teams for creation, curation, maintenance, and governance
