# How to create a UI/UX portfolio

A UI/UX portfolio should showcase your skills, tell a compelling story of your design journey, and demonstrate your ability to solve real-world problems through effective UI/UX design. Before finalizing, seek feedback from peers, mentors, or professionals in the field.

- Showcase Your Best Work: select impressive, diverse projects across styles, platforms, devices, and industries
- Provide Case Studies: detail the problem, design process, solutions, and design decisions per project
- Highlight Your Role: clearly state your role and contributions in each project (UI design, UX research, interaction design, etc.)
- Include Visual Assets: high-quality screenshots, mockups, prototypes, interactive elements, and animations
- Demonstrate User-Centered Design: show user research, feedback gathering, and iteration
- Display Your Design Process: sketches, wireframes, and iterations
- Highlight Collaboration: experience working with developers, product managers, or other designers
