# Google Material Design

Google Material Design is a set of guidelines developed by Google to create visually appealing and consistent user interfaces across different platforms and devices. It emphasizes a clean and modern design aesthetic while also considering functionality and usability.

- Material Metaphor: tangible surface using depth, shadows, and animations for hierarchy
- Bold Intentional Typography: typographic hierarchy emphasizing readability and clarity
- Grid-based Layouts: responsive grid system for structure and consistency
- Color and Contrast: vibrant bold palette, color to highlight, high contrast for accessibility
- Responsive Design: adaptable to different screen sizes and orientations
- Motion: animation to provide feedback, guide attention, and enhance experience
- Consistency and Continuity: standardized components, interactions, and patterns
