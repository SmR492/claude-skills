Mockups are visual representations of a design or product concept, used across software development, product design, and marketing. They can take many forms such as sketches, wireframes, digital prototypes, or physical models. Created early in the design process, they help stakeholders visualize and test design ideas, communicate concepts, gather feedback, and identify potential problems before investing in a full-scale product.

- In software development: visualize the UI and application flow, from low-fidelity sketches to high-fidelity prototypes
- Used to test different user interactions and workflows before coding
- In product design: test physical prototypes (cardboard, foam, 3D printing) for feasibility and usability
- In marketing: create visual representations for advertising, social media, or print to generate interest
