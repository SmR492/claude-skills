# Accessibility

Accessibility refers to the design and development of products, services, environments, or digital content that can be used by individuals with disabilities, ensuring everyone can access and engage with information, technology, and physical spaces regardless of their abilities. Benefits include improved user experience, inclusivity, legal and ethical compliance, expanded audience, and future-proofing. Ensuring it requires following established guidelines such as WCAG and conducting accessibility testing and audits.

- Perceivability: information perceivable through multiple senses (alt text, captions, readable text, color contrast)
- Operability: interfaces operable by different abilities (keyboard-only access, screen readers, ARIA, assistive technologies)
- Understandability: clear content and functionality (plain language, logical organization, guidance)
- Robustness: interpretable by a wide range of assistive tech, devices, and platforms (web standards, alt text attributes, semantic markup)
