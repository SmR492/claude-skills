# Focus group

A focus group is a qualitative research method used to gather opinions and attitudes from a small, diverse group of individuals about a particular product, service, concept, or topic. It typically involves 6 to 10 individuals representing the target audience, asked open-ended questions or given tasks, with discussion led by a moderator. It is often used in market research before launch to identify consumer needs, preferences, and potential issues.

- Typically 6 to 10 participants representing the target audience
- A moderator guides the conversation and ensures participation
- Advantages: detailed in-depth information and insight into reasons behind consumer behavior
- Limitations: may not represent wider population, group dynamics and moderator can influence results, selection/question bias
