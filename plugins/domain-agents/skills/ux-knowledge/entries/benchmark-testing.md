Benchmark testing (benchmarking) compares the performance of a computer system, software application, or technology against a standard or reference point. Its goal is to evaluate speed, efficiency, and overall performance and to identify areas for improvement; appropriate benchmarks and methods must be chosen and results interpreted carefully, as they may not reflect real-world performance.

- Performance testing: test performance under different conditions and workloads
- Performance regression testing: test performance after changes or updates
- Load testing: test ability to handle a large amount of traffic or activity
- Stress testing: test ability to handle extreme conditions
- Compatibility testing: test compatibility with different OSes, devices, or software environments
