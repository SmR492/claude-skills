# How to get feedback

Getting feedback is essential for personal and professional growth. The excerpt outlines steps from being open and approachable to following up for clarification.

- Be Open and Approachable: be open-minded and receptive to different perspectives
- Ask Different Sources: seek feedback from supervisors, colleagues, mentors, peers, customers, or clients
- Be Specific: be clear about the areas or aspects you want feedback on
- Ask Open-Ended Questions: encourage detailed responses rather than yes/no answers
- Listen Actively: listen without interrupting or becoming defensive
- Respond Graciously: express appreciation regardless of whether feedback is positive or constructive
- Reflect and Apply the Feedback: reflect, align with self-assessment and goals, identify areas to improve
- Follow Up: reach out for clarification on feedback you don't fully understand
