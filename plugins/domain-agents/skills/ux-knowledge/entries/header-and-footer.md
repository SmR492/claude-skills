# Header and footer

A header and footer are two essential components of a website's layout that appear consistently across multiple pages. The header sits at the top and typically contains branding, logo, and primary navigation, while the footer sits at the bottom and often contains secondary navigation, copyright and legal information, and links to important pages.

- Header: branding, logo, primary navigation; may include search bars, contact info, social icons
- Footer: secondary navigation, copyright, legal disclaimers, links (privacy policy/terms), contact details
- Consistency: appear across all pages, aiding navigation
- Aesthetics: contribute to overall design and a cohesive experience
- SEO and Conversion Opportunities: keywords, internal linking, CTAs, and sign-up links
