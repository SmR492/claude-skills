# Business lingo

A reference skill, not a worker. It replaces the former lingo-translator, biz-analyst and principle-guard roles.

## Rules

1. Check the phrase or framework against `entries/INDEX.md` (ref, title, file). Read the matching entry and quote it; say "not covered" when no entry's title carries the phrase or an inflection of it. Do not explain from memory as if it were the pack.
2. Explain in English; German on request. Never map a German idiom silently onto an English entry.
3. Mail, meeting or customer text given by the user stays in the conversation; only the phrase itself is looked up.
4. Entries are bulk extractions and marked unverified; figures and legal statements in them are starting points, not sources.
5. Framework pick (former biz-analyst): classify the problem (strategy, prioritisation, root cause, planning, structure, decision) and name the matching entries from `frameworks.md`; where the palette has no entry (PESTLE, stakeholder analysis, process mapping, use-case analysis), say so instead of improvising a pseudo-structure.
6. Lenses (former principle-guard): `lenses.md` lists the laws and heuristics with the situations they apply to. They are heuristics, not laws that can be "violated"; a verdict is "applies, with locator in the plan, and no countermeasure", never "violation" by itself.

## Files

- `entries/INDEX.md`, `entries/*.md`: the 89 BL entries, generated by `scripts/build-packs.mjs`.
- `frameworks.md`: problem type to framework mapping.
- `lenses.md`: the lens checklist for plans.
