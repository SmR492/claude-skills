# Unknown unknowns

"Unknown unknowns" describes risks, issues, or challenges that are not only unknown but also not anticipated or recognized. The phrase was popularized by former U.S. Secretary of Defense Donald Rumsfeld in a 2002 news briefing distinguishing known knowns, known unknowns, and unknown unknowns. It is a reminder that factors beyond current knowledge can impact outcomes, and mitigation requires humility, curiosity, and openness.

- Relevant in project management, risk assessment, and strategy development
- Mitigation techniques: scenario planning, comprehensive risk assessments
- Seeking diverse perspectives and a learning-oriented culture
