# The Law of Demos (Kapor's Law)

The Law of Demos, also known as Kapor's Law, states that any technology demo will eventually fail if it is demonstrated often enough. It was first formulated by Mitch Kapor, co-founder of Lotus Development Corporation, in 1983. Demos are controlled, essentially fake environments that showcase the best features while glossing over flaws, so the more a demo is shown the more likely its limitations become apparent. A recommended solution is to be transparent about a technology's limitations and challenges, even during a demo.
