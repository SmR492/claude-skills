# Win-win

Ref: `BL:win-win` · Category: term · Entry version: 1

"Win-win" is a term in business and negotiations describing a situation where all parties involved benefit from a decision or agreement, a mutually beneficial outcome as opposed to a zero-sum game where one's gain is another's loss. Each party's goals and needs are considered and addressed. Win-win solutions can be difficult when parties have conflicting goals, but focusing on common interests and creative solutions often makes them possible.
