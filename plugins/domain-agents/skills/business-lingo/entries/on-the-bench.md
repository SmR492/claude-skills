# On the bench

"On the bench" is a term used in project management, software development, and other work contexts to refer to an employee who is not currently assigned to a specific project or task but is available and waiting to be assigned. It derives from sports, where players not actively playing are "on the bench." It can be challenging for employees who feel underutilized, but also offers a chance to develop skills or train; for management it is both a cost and a source of flexible, available talent.
