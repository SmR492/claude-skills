# The ball is in your court

Ref: `BL:the-ball-is-in-your-court` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 51. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-the-ball-is-in-your-court.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

"The ball is in your court" is an idiom meaning it is your turn to take action or make a decision, often used when one party in a negotiation has made a proposal and it is up to the other to respond. Its origin is believed to come from tennis, where the ball is hit back and forth. It is a polite way to shift responsibility and create a sense of urgency for the person addressed to act.
