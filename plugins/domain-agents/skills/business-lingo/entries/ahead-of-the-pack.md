# Ahead of the pack

Ref: `BL:ahead-of-the-pack` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 28. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-ahead-of-the-pack.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

The idiom "ahead of the pack" refers to being in a leading or advantageous position compared to others in a competitive context. It suggests being ahead or superior in performance, achievement, skills, or innovation, standing out from the competition and being at the forefront in terms of quality, progress, or success.

- Sports and Competitions: athletes or teams leading or outperforming competitors
- Business and Innovation: companies or individuals who are innovative and surpass industry peers in growth, market share, or ideas
- Academics and Education: students or researchers who excel and set themselves apart from peers
- Personal and Professional Development: individuals who have achieved notable success or demonstrated exceptional skills
