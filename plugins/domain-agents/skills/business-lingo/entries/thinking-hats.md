# Thinking Hats

Thinking Hats is a decision-making and problem-solving technique that uses the metaphor of hats to encourage different ways of thinking, with each hat representing a distinct type of thinking. By wearing a particular hat, individuals are encouraged to think in a particular way. It can be used from individual problem-solving to group decision-making and can improve communication, creativity, and decision-making.

- White Hat: objective, factual thinking — what information is available and needed
- Red Hat: emotional thinking — instincts, feelings, intuitions
- Black Hat: critical thinking — risks and potential problems
- Yellow Hat: optimistic thinking — benefits and positive aspects
- Green Hat: creative thinking — generating new ideas and possibilities
- Blue Hat: meta-cognitive thinking — overall process, structure, and organization of the session
