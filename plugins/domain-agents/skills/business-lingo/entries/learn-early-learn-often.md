# Learn early, learn often

Ref: `BL:learn-early-learn-often` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 83. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-learn-early-learn-often.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

A quotation by Drew Houston, co-founder of Dropbox, popular in the startup community for emphasizing continuous learning and experimentation. It suggests starting to learn and experiment early in a product's development rather than waiting until changes are harder and more expensive. It is closely tied to the lean startup methodology's rapid experimentation and iteration, validating or invalidating assumptions and making data-driven decisions.

- Quotation by Drew Houston (Dropbox)
- Tied to the lean startup methodology (rapid experimentation and iteration)
- 'Learn': acquire knowledge via experimentation, feedback and data
- 'Early': begin learning as soon as possible, even before launch
- 'Often': continuous learning and iteration throughout development
