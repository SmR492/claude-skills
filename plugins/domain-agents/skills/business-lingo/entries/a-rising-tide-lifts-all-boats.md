# A rising tide lifts all boats

Ref: `BL:a-rising-tide-lifts-all-boats` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 79. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-a-rising-tide-lifts-all-boats.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

A metaphorical expression conveying that when an overall environment or economic condition improves, it benefits everyone involved regardless of individual circumstances or position. Its origin is often attributed to John F. Kennedy, who used it in a 1963 speech to stress that an improving economy benefits all members of society. Beyond economics it can describe the positive impact of collective effort or a favorable environment, though it does not guarantee that everyone benefits equally.

- General positive trend benefits all participants in a domain
- Often attributed to John F. Kennedy (1963 speech)
- Applies beyond economics to collaboration and collective effort
- Does not guarantee everyone benefits equally
