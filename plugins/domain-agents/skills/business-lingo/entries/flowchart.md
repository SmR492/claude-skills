A flowchart is a graphical representation of a process, system, or workflow that uses symbols and arrows to depict the sequence of steps or actions and how information or materials flow from one step to another. Flowcharts are widely used in business, engineering, programming, and project management to visually represent complex processes and facilitate understanding.

- Start/End: rounded rectangle or oval marking the starting or end point
- Process Step: rectangles or squares for individual steps or actions
- Decision: diamond-shaped symbols for decision points or branches
- Connector: small circles or dots connecting different parts of the flowchart
- Arrow: connects symbols and indicates the flow/direction of the process
- Input/Output: parallelograms for where data or information enters or leaves
