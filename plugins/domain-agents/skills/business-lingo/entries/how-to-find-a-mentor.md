# How to find a mentor

Finding a mentor, advisor, or coach can be a valuable step in personal and professional growth. Steps for identifying and connecting with a mentor.

- Define your goals to identify the right mentor
- Ask your network (colleagues, supervisors, teachers, industry contacts) for leads
- Make connections with a polite, concise message explaining why you admire their work
- Consider formal mentorship programs from organizations, institutions, or associations
- Build relationships by interacting with professionals in your field
- Be open to guidance from a diverse range of sources
- Be growth-oriented: seek advice, request feedback, and show dedication to growth
