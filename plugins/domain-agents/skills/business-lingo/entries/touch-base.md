# Touch base

Ref: `BL:touch-base` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 54. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-touch-base.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

The idiom "touch base" means to make contact or communicate briefly with someone to get an update, share information, or establish a connection. It originated from baseball, where a runner must touch each base to score a run. It is commonly used in business, professional, and social contexts where individuals or teams periodically check in and coordinate.

- Communication: reaching out to exchange information or establish contact
- Brief and Informal: a short informal conversation, not a formal meeting
- Update or Alignment: sharing updates and getting on the same page
- Connectivity: maintaining regular contact
