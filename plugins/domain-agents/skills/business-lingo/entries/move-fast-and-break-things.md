# Move fast and break things

Ref: `BL:move-fast-and-break-things` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 87. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-move-fast-and-break-things.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

A phrase coined by Mark Zuckerberg, founder of Facebook, whose idea is that companies should prioritize speed and innovation over avoiding mistakes or failures, encouraging risk-taking and experimentation. It is associated with Silicon Valley startup culture focused on disrupting industries through rapid development. It has been criticized for potential negative impacts on users and society and for encouraging reckless behavior, and in recent years has fallen out of favor as companies shift toward more deliberate approaches focused on user safety, privacy and sustainability.

- Coined by Mark Zuckerberg (Facebook)
- Prioritize speed and innovation over avoiding mistakes
- Associated with Silicon Valley disruption culture
- Criticized for negative impact on users/society and reckless behavior
- Fallen out of favor; shift toward deliberate, responsible practices
