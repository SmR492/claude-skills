# Big picture

Ref: `BL:big-picture` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 30. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-big-picture.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

"Big picture" is an idiom referring to the overall or broader perspective of a situation, concept, or problem. It implies looking at things from a wider viewpoint rather than getting caught up in specific details or immediate concerns, focusing on the larger context, long-term goals, and the connections and implications of various elements. It encourages stepping back to understand the greater significance of a situation.

- Gain perspective: a clearer understanding of the situation, its complexities and long-term implications
- Make informed decisions: decisions aligned with goals that have a positive long-run impact
- Identify priorities: pinpoint the most important elements within a situation or project
- Plan strategically: develop strategic plans that consider broader context and future developments
