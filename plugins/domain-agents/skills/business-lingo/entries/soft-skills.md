# Soft skills

Also known as interpersonal skills or people skills: the personal attributes and qualities that enable individuals to effectively interact with others and navigate various social and professional situations.

- Communication: articulating ideas effectively, verbally and in writing (active listening, clarity, empathy, adaptability)
- Collaboration: working well with others and building positive relationships (cooperation, compromise, constructive conflict handling)
- Leadership: guiding, motivating, and inspiring others toward a common goal
- Adaptability: flexibility and willingness to adjust to changing circumstances, environments, or tasks
- Emotional intelligence: understanding and managing one's own emotions and empathizing with others'
- Time management: prioritizing tasks, setting goals, and managing time efficiently
- Creativity: thinking creatively, generating innovative ideas, and taking creative risks
