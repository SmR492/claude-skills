# Growth-Share Matrix

The Growth-Share Matrix, also known as the Boston Consulting Group (BCG) matrix, is a strategic planning tool used to analyze and categorize a product portfolio based on market growth rate and relative market share. It classifies products into four quadrants, each with its own strategic implications, providing a framework for managing growth, cash flow, and investment decisions.

- Stars: high market growth rate and high relative market share
- Cash Cows: low growth and high share; low investment, a source of funds
- Questions: high growth and low share; potential but risky, requires careful consideration
- Dogs: low growth and low share; minimal profit, candidates for divestment
