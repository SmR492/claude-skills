# How to manage up

Managing up is the practice of proactively working with your supervisor or manager to build a strong and productive working relationship. Practices for aligning with and supporting your manager.

- Adapt your style to your manager's communication preferences and decision-making processes
- Build trust through reliability, quality work, and a proactive, collaborative attitude
- Communicate clearly about progress, challenges, and support you need
- Align your work and objectives with your manager's and the organization's goals
- Anticipate needs and take initiative to offer assistance before being asked
- Seek feedback on performance and act on it
- Manage expectations about deliverables, deadlines, and quality standards
- Be solutions-oriented, coming prepared with potential options
- Foster collaboration with colleagues
- Continuously develop your skills and knowledge
