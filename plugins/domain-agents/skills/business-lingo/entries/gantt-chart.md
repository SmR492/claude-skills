# Gantt chart

A Gantt chart is a horizontal bar chart used in project management to visually represent the progress of a project over time, named after its creator Henry Gantt, who introduced the charting technique in the early 1900s. It displays a project timeline divided into tasks, where each task is a horizontal bar whose length corresponds to its duration and whose position shows its start and end dates.

- Horizontal axis represents project duration; vertical axis represents individual tasks
- Can plan and track projects from small to large, complex ones with interdependent tasks
- Particularly useful for identifying critical path tasks that must finish on time to keep the project on schedule
- Commonly used in project management software and in presentations/reports to communicate status to stakeholders
