# Make mistakes faster

Ref: `BL:make-mistakes-faster` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 84. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-make-mistakes-faster.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

A quote from Andy Grove, former CEO of Intel, used to emphasize the importance of taking risks and being willing to fail in order to achieve success. The idea is that the faster you make mistakes, the faster you can learn from them and improve, rather than dwelling on them and wasting time. It is particularly relevant to entrepreneurs and innovators and is closely related to the idea of 'fail fast, fail often.'

- Quote from Andy Grove (former Intel CEO)
- Faster mistakes lead to faster learning and improvement
- Encourages risk-taking and iterating quickly
- Closely related to 'fail fast, fail often'
