# SWOT analysis

SWOT analysis is a strategic planning tool that helps businesses identify their strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats. It is often used to assess a current position, develop a strategy, and make informed decisions. Once complete, the business uses the insights to build a strategy that leverages strengths, addresses weaknesses, seizes opportunities, and mitigates threats.

- Strengths: internal factors giving an advantage (e.g. strong brand, loyal customers, unique product, talented employees, efficient processes)
- Weaknesses: internal factors hindering success (e.g. outdated technology, poor controls, insufficient resources, low morale)
- Opportunities: external factors to capitalize on (e.g. emerging markets, changing consumer behavior, technological advances, new partnerships)
- Threats: external factors that negatively affect the business (e.g. increased competition, economic downturns, obstructionist regulation, natural disasters)
