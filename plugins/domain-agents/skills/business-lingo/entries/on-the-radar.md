# On the radar

Ref: `BL:on-the-radar` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 39. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-on-the-radar.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

"On the radar" is an idiom meaning that something or someone is being monitored, watched, or considered for future reference or action, drawn from a radar system that detects and tracks objects over time. It is commonly used in business settings when discussing potential opportunities or risks, and can describe a person or company gaining prominence worth keeping an eye on for future developments or collaboration.
