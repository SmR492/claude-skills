# Over the horizon

Ref: `BL:over-the-horizon` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 41. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-over-the-horizon.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

"Over the horizon" is an idiomatic expression referring to events, situations, or possibilities that are not currently visible or known but are anticipated to occur in the future, implying something lies beyond the current range of perception. Drawn from the visual concept of the horizon as the farthest visible point, it conveys looking or thinking ahead, anticipating developments, and preparing for challenges or opportunities.

- Strategic planning: considering long-term trends, technological advances, and potential disruptions with a forward-looking perspective
- Military and defense: activities, threats, or operations beyond the visible range of radar or surveillance
- Everyday conversation: future plans, aspirations, or changes not yet visible but expected to materialize
