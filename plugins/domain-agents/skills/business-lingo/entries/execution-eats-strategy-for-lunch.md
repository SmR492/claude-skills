# Execution eats strategy for lunch

Ref: `BL:execution-eats-strategy-for-lunch` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 76. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-execution-eats-strategy-for-lunch.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

A popular business saying emphasizing that execution and implementation matter for success even more than having a great strategy. It is often attributed to Peter Drucker, though there is no record of him saying it. Having a great strategy is important but not enough; success also requires the ability to execute effectively - being agile, adaptable and quick to respond to change. Successful execution requires the right people, processes and tools, plus effective prioritization, clear communication and efficient resource management.

- Often (mis)attributed to Peter Drucker; no record he said it
- Strategy alone is not enough; execution is essential
- Requires the right people, processes and tools
- Involves prioritizing, communicating clearly and managing resources efficiently
- Encourages being proactive, nimble and results-focused
