# Conway's law

Ref: `BL:conways-law` · Category: law · Entry version: 1

Conway's law is a principle in software engineering stating that the structure of a software system reflects the communication structure of the organization that produced it. It was first proposed by Melvin Conway in 1968: organizations that design systems are constrained to produce designs that copy their communication structures. It implies systems should be designed to reflect desired communication and collaboration structures, e.g. via cross-functional teams, and highlights the importance of organizational culture.
