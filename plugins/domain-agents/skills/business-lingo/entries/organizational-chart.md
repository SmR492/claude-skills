# Organizational chart (org chart)

An organizational chart, or org chart for short, is a visual representation of a company's structure and hierarchy. It shows the relationships between different positions and departments as well as the reporting relationships between employees, typically displaying top-level executives at the top with subsequent levels of management and staff below. Org charts help employees understand their roles, help managers spot overlaps or gaps in responsibility, and support planning such as reorganization or restructuring.

- Hierarchical org chart: most common type, shows a clear chain of command with each level reporting to the one above
- Matrix org chart: shows relationships between employees on different projects or departments and may lack a clear chain of command
- Can be created with tools like Microsoft PowerPoint, Visio, or specialized org chart software
- Displayed on intranets or printed materials such as employee handbooks or training manuals
