A mind map is a graphical tool used to organize and structure ideas and information visually. It starts with a central idea placed in the center and branches out to related ideas or subtopics. Mind maps are often used for brainstorming, problem-solving, note-taking, and organizing information, for personal or professional purposes.

- Better organization: structures information in a logical, easy-to-understand way
- Increased creativity: encourages brainstorming and free association
- Improved memory retention: uses visual and spatial relationships
- Enhanced communication: conveys complex ideas simply and concisely
- Create on paper or with software: central idea in the center, branches radiating out, enhanced with color, images, and symbols
