# Brooks' Law

Brooks' Law is a principle in software development stating that adding more people to a late project only makes it later. It was named after Fred Brooks, who described it in "The Mythical Man-Month: Essays on Software Engineering" (1975). The law is based on the observation that adding people to a late project decreases productivity due to communication overhead and onboarding time; Brooks suggested improving the process, removing obstacles, and increasing existing team productivity instead.
