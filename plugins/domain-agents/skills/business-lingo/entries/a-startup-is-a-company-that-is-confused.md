# A startup is a company that is confused

A quotation by Dave McClure, co-founder of 500 Startups: 'A startup is a company that is confused about 1. What its product is. 2. Who its customers are. 3. How to make money.' It highlights the main challenges startups face in their early stages and the uncertainty and ambiguity inherent to that phase. It stresses the importance of quickly iterating and experimenting to find the right product-market fit and revenue model.

- What its product is: define the offering so it resonates with customers (experimentation and iteration)
- Who its customers are: identify specific customer segments willing to pay (market research and customer discovery)
- How to make money: find the best revenue model (experiment with pricing and revenue models)
- Emphasizes rapid iteration to find product-market fit and revenue model
