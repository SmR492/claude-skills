# How to work with stakeholders

Working with stakeholders is crucial for successful project execution and desired outcomes, requiring active engagement, effective communication, and a genuine commitment to understanding and addressing their needs.

- Prioritize stakeholders (internal, external, clients, sponsors, end-users, regulators) based on level of impact
- Understand expectations via stakeholder analysis of goals, motivations, and risks
- Communicate regularly and transparently about expectations, progress, challenges, and decisions
- Build relationships based on trust, reliability, and follow-through
- Involve stakeholders in decision-making (workshops, focus groups, collaborations)
- Resolve conflicts as a mediator, addressing them early before they escalate
- Provide value through showcases, demos, and updates aligned to their objectives
- Be adaptable to evolving stakeholder needs and priorities
