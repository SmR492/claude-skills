# How to negotiate

Negotiation is a skill honed with practice and preparation; it is a dynamic process where flexibility matters, adapting the approach based on circumstances, the other party's behavior, and new information arising during the negotiation.

- Prepare: define and prioritize goals, research the other party, and identify leverage points
- Build rapport through active listening, respect, and open communication
- Set the agenda collaboratively and define negotiation parameters (scope, timeframe, outcomes)
- Bargain for win-win outcomes, focusing on interests not positions, and look for value-creating trade-offs
- Overcome obstacles with a collaborative mindset and by managing emotions calmly
- Reach a mutually beneficial agreement, documenting terms and seeking necessary approvals
