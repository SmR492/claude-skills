# How to get feedback

Getting feedback is essential for personal and professional growth. Practices for soliciting and responding to feedback effectively.

- Be open and approachable, receptive to different perspectives
- Ask different sources (supervisors, colleagues, mentors, peers, customers, clients)
- Be specific about the areas you want feedback on
- Ask open-ended questions that encourage detailed responses
- Listen actively without interrupting or becoming defensive
- Respond graciously and express appreciation regardless of whether it is positive or constructive
- Reflect and apply the feedback, identifying areas to improve
- Follow up for clarification when needed
