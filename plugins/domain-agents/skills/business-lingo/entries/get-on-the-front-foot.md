# Get on the front foot

Ref: `BL:get-on-the-front-foot` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 34. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-get-on-the-front-foot.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

"Get on the front foot" means to take a proactive approach to a situation rather than waiting for something to happen and reacting to it, often used to encourage being assertive and taking initiative. It derives from sports, particularly football (soccer), where an attacking team is "on the front foot" and controls the pace and direction of the game. In business it means anticipating problems or opportunities and acting before they become critical, and can also mean taking a leadership role.
