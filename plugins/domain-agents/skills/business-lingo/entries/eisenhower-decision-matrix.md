# Eisenhower Decision Matrix

The Eisenhower Decision Matrix is a time management and productivity tool named after Dwight D. Eisenhower, the 34th President of the United States. It helps individuals prioritize tasks using four quadrants formed by two axes, urgency and importance. The goal is to focus on important tasks and avoid being overwhelmed by urgent but less significant activities.

- Urgent + Important = Do First (deadlines, emergencies, critical issues)
- Not Urgent + Important = Schedule (planning, skill development, relationship building, goal setting)
- Urgent + Not Important = Delegate (certain meetings, routine administrative tasks)
- Not Urgent + Not Important = Eliminate (distractions, excessive social media, unnecessary meetings)
