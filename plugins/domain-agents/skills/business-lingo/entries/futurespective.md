# Futurespective

A futurespective is a group activity that focuses on exploring and envisioning possible futures for a team, organization, or project. Its main goal is to imagine a range of possible future scenarios and use them to inform current decision-making and planning. It typically involves a group of people and is often facilitated by a trained facilitator or coach.

- Participants imagine scenarios such as best-case and worst-case outcomes and the factors leading to them
- Uses tools such as brainstorming, scenario planning, and SWOT analysis
- Encourages thinking creatively and challenging assumptions about the future
- Especially useful for projects with high uncertainty, such as new product development or strategic planning
