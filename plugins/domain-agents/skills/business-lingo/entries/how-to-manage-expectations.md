# How to manage expectations

Managing expectations is essential for maintaining strong relationships, avoiding misunderstandings, and achieving successful outcomes. Practices for setting and maintaining realistic expectations.

- Be clear: articulate goals, deliverables, timelines, constraints, and limitations
- Be realistic and aligned with scope and resources; avoid overpromising
- Confirm communications in writing (charters, scope statements, contracts)
- Provide regular updates, status reports, and progress notes
- Manage changes with a defined process, assessing impact on timeline, budget, and resources
- Provide rationale and context for decisions and changes
- Involve stakeholders in relevant decision-making
- Address issues promptly and directly through open, respectful discussion
- Manage risk with contingency plans for unexpected events
