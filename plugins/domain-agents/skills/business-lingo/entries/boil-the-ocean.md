# Boil the ocean

"Boil the ocean" is an idiomatic expression describing a task or project so complex, difficult, or extensive that it is virtually impossible to complete. In business it describes an overly ambitious or broad-scope effort, or trying to solve all problems at once without clear priorities or realistic resources. It is a cautionary phrase stressing the importance of focusing on achievable goals and breaking complex projects into smaller, manageable tasks and incremental steps.
