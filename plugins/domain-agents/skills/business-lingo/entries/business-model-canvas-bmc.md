# Business Model Canvas (BMC)

The Business Model Canvas (BMC) is a visual tool that helps entrepreneurs and businesses describe, design, and analyze their business model. It identifies the most important components of a business model and how they interrelate, guiding decisions about resources, offerings, customers, and growth, and is also useful for analyzing competitors' business models to find areas of differentiation.

- Customer Segments: to whom the business aims to sell
- Value Proposition: how the business helps customer needs and wants via products or services
- Channels: ways the business reaches customers and delivers its value proposition
- Customer Relationships: how the business relates to customers (personal assistance, self-service, automated)
- Revenue Streams: ways the business generates revenue (product sales, subscriptions, advertising)
- Key Resources: assets, people, and resources required
- Key Activities: activities required to operate the business
- Key Partnerships: relationships with other businesses or organizations
- Cost Structure: costs and fees associated with operating the business
