# How to give a demo

Giving a demo involves showcasing a product, service, or concept to an audience, whether in-person, virtual, or a combination. Steps for planning and delivering an engaging demo.

- Know your audience and tailor the demo to their needs and pain points
- Set objectives (generate interest, educate, or make a sale)
- Plan and prepare content, messages, and visuals with a storyboard; practice
- Be engaging with an attention-grabbing introduction, then focus on unique aspects and benefits
- Be dynamic, varying tone, pace, and gestures, and consider interactivity
- Use visuals such as slides, videos, and walkthroughs
- Tell stories with real-life examples, case studies, or testimonials
- Address concerns and invite questions during or after sections
- End with a call to action, summarizing key points and benefits
- Follow up and encourage feedback to nurture the relationship
