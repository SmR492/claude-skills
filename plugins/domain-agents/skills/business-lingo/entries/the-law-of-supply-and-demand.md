# The Law of Supply and Demand

The Law of Supply and Demand is an economic principle explaining how the price and quantity of goods and services in a market are determined by the balance between supply and demand. When demand exceeds supply, price tends to rise; when supply exceeds demand, price tends to fall. The intersection of the supply and demand curves determines the equilibrium price and quantity, where quantity supplied equals quantity demanded.

- Law of Supply: the higher the price, the greater the quantity suppliers will produce and offer
- Law of Demand: the lower the price, the greater the quantity buyers will demand
- Equilibrium: intersection of supply and demand curves sets price and quantity
