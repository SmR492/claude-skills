# Have a think

Ref: `BL:have-a-think` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 35. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-have-a-think.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

"Have a think" is an idiomatic expression meaning to take some time to reflect or consider something carefully, often used to encourage someone to ponder an idea or problem before making a decision or taking action. It is a polite, encouraging way to promote careful decision-making and thoughtful analysis, common in business contexts and also in casual settings. It is similar to "think it over," "consider it," or "mull it over."
