# Shoulder to shoulder

Ref: `BL:shoulder-to-shoulder` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 46. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-shoulder-to-shoulder.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

The idiom "shoulder to shoulder" describes people standing or working closely together, side by side, often with a shared purpose or goal. It conveys unity, collaboration, and mutual support, originating from the physical act of standing with shoulder touching shoulder. In a broader sense it can refer to providing emotional support or standing together in solidarity during challenging times.
