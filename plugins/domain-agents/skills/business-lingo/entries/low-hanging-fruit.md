# Low-hanging fruit

Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 37. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-low-hanging-fruit.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

"Low-hanging fruit" is a metaphorical term describing tasks, actions, or opportunities that are easily achievable, require minimal effort or resources, and provide immediate and significant benefits. It comes from fruit-picking, where easily accessible fruit is harvested first while higher, harder-to-reach fruit is left for later. While it offers quick, easy wins, one should not focus solely on it and neglect other important goals, and should ensure it aligns with overall strategy.

- Software Development: quick, easy tasks with meaningful impact (e.g. fixing a minor issue, improving a feature, optimizing a process)
- Targeted Marketing: easily accessible customer segments or markets with high growth potential
- Risk Management: opportunities to mitigate significant risks (e.g. fixing a minor security vulnerability, basic safety measures)
