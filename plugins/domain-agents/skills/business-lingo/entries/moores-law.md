# Moore's Law

Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 64. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-moores-law.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

Moore's Law is a prediction made by Gordon Moore, co-founder of Intel Corporation, in 1965. He originally observed that the number of transistors on an integrated circuit would double roughly every year, revising this in 1975 to about every two years, while the cost per transistor decreased, leading to increased computing power and decreased cost of technology. It has proven remarkably accurate, driving innovation and competition. Some experts believe it may be approaching its limits as transistor size nears the atomic scale and development costs increase.
