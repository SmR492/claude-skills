# Perfect is the enemy of good

Ref: `BL:perfect-is-the-enemy-of-good` · Category: principle · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 85. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-perfect-is-the-enemy-of-good.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

An aphorism emphasizing the potential negative impact of striving for perfection, suggesting that pursuing perfection can hinder progress and prevent satisfactory results. The quest for perfection often sets unrealistic standards and can breed dissatisfaction and never-ending refinement. Pursuing perfection also consumes valuable time, resources and energy with diminishing returns. The aphorism encourages a pragmatic approach that recognizes and appreciates the goodness in what is already achieved.

- Perfectionism sets unrealistic standards and becomes a barrier to success
- Pursuing perfection consumes time, resources and energy with diminishing returns
- Fixating on details can cause missed opportunities for growth or task completion
- Embracing 'good' leads to greater satisfaction, productivity and adaptability
