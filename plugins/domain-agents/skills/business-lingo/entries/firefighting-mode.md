# Firefighting mode

"Firefighting mode" is a term used in project management and organizational contexts to describe a reactive state where individuals or teams scramble to address urgent or unexpected issues rather than working proactively toward long-term goals. The focus is on the immediate crisis, often at the expense of other priorities, and it can be stressful. While sometimes necessary, it can also signal deeper issues such as unclear priorities, thin resources, or a culture of putting out fires rather than preventing them.

- Avoid it by identifying and addressing root causes before problems escalate
- Establish clear priorities and processes for managing urgent issues
- Foster a culture of proactive problem-solving and continuous improvement
