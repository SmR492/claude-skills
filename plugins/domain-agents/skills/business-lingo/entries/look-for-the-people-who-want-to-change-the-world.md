# Look for the people who want to change the world

Ref: `BL:look-for-the-people-who-want-to-change-the-world` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 80. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-look-for-the-people-who-want-to-change-the-world.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

A phrase often associated with Salesforce, a leading CRM software company, representing its commitment to hiring people passionate about making a difference. At its core it is a statement about the importance of values alignment in the workplace, seeking individuals driven by purpose to build a culture focused on the mission of 'making the world a better place.' Salesforce reinforces this through its 1-1-1 model - donating 1% of equity, 1% of employees' time and 1% of products to charitable causes.

- Associated with Salesforce (CRM company)
- About values alignment in the workplace
- Emphasis on hiring people committed to social and environmental causes
- Reinforced by the 1-1-1 model: 1% equity, 1% employee time, 1% products to charity
