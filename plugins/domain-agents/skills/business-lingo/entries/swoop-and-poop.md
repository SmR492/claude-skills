# Swoop and poop

Ref: `BL:swoop-and-poop` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 49. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-swoop-and-poop.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

"Swoop and poop" is a slang term in business and project management for a situation where an individual or team enters a project at the last minute and makes critical comments or decisions that undermine work already done. The "swoop" is the sudden unexpected entrance and the "poop" is the negative impact. It can also describe a manager or executive who swoops in and takes credit for others' work; it is avoided through regular open communication and involving all stakeholders from the outset.
