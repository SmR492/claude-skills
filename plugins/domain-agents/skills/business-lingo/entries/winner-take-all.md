# Winner take all

Ref: `BL:winner-take-all` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 57. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-winner-take-all.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

"Winner-take-all" refers to a market situation in which one player or company captures a significant majority of the market share while smaller players are left with little or none. The dominant player captures a disproportionate share of profits and can be hard for competitors to displace once it has captured share, as it can invest resources in R&D and marketing. The phenomenon occurs in industries such as technology, finance, and entertainment.

- Network effects: a product becomes more valuable as more people use it
- Economies of scale: costs fall as the company grows larger
- Brand recognition: consumers' familiarity and trust in a brand
