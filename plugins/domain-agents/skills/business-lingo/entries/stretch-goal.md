# Stretch goal

Ref: `BL:stretch-goal` · Category: technique · Entry version: 1

A "stretch goal" is an ambitious goal set beyond what is normally expected or easily achievable, used to motivate individuals, teams, or organizations to perform beyond perceived capabilities and drive innovation and creativity. Such goals should be tied to the organization's strategy and vision. If unrealistic or unattainable, they can cause demotivation, frustration, and burnout, so a balance between challenging and achievable is needed.

- Used in project management, business planning, and personal development
- In project management: complete ahead of schedule, under budget, or with higher quality
- Risk: unrealistic goals lead to demotivation, frustration, burnout
