# Aphorisms

Aphorisms are concise, memorable, and often witty statements that convey a general truth or principle, offering succinct insights into human nature and life. The term originates from the Greek "aphorismos," meaning "definition" or "distinction." Their characteristics include brevity, clarity, and universality, and they serve to provide guidance, inspire reflection, provoke thought, or offer practical advice; their brevity can also leave room for multiple interpretations.
