# Idioms

Ref: `BL:idioms` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 27. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-idioms.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

Idioms are phrases or expressions whose meaning differs from the literal meaning of the words used, commonly used in everyday language to add color or emphasis. Their meaning cannot be understood by translating the individual words; instead they are understood through usage and context, which makes them difficult for non-native speakers or those unfamiliar with the language or culture.

- Example: "the ball is in your court" means it is now someone's turn or responsibility to act
- Example: "barking up the wrong tree" means pursuing a mistaken or misguided course of action
