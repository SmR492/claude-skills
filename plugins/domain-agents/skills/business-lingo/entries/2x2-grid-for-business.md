# 2x2 grid for business

A 2x2 grid commonly used in business for strategic analysis or decision-making, assessing market attractiveness against competitive strength. It provides a framework to evaluate market opportunities and competitive advantages and to make strategic decisions about resource allocation, growth strategies, and market positioning.

- Quadrant 1 Invest/Grow: high market attractiveness + strong competitive position
- Quadrant 2 Protect/Harvest: high market attractiveness + weak competitive position
- Quadrant 3 Build/Exit: low market attractiveness + strong competitive position
- Quadrant 4 Divest/Retire: low market attractiveness + weak competitive position
