# Good Enough For Now (GEFN)

Good Enough For Now (GEFN) is a concept describing a standard of quality or completeness that is adequate for the immediate needs of a particular situation. Often used in software development, it describes a solution sufficient to meet current requirements but that may require further refinement later. It is rooted in iterative development and is often used in agile methodologies.

- Encourages delivering functional and reliable code quickly rather than striving for perfection
- Focus on delivering the most critical features first, leaving room for future enhancements
- Must balance speed and agility with quality and maintainability
- Can lead to technical debt, making software harder and costlier to maintain over time
