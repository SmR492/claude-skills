# Think outside of the box

Ref: `BL:think-outside-of-the-box` · Category: technique · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 53. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-think-outside-of-the-box.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

"Think outside of the box" describes approaching a problem or situation in an unconventional, creative, or innovative way, thinking beyond traditional ideas, methods, and processes. The phrase originated from the 1960s "nine dots puzzle," whose solution required drawing lines outside the perceived boundary of the square. It requires cultivating a mindset of creativity, curiosity, open-mindedness, risk-taking, and willingness to experiment and iterate.
