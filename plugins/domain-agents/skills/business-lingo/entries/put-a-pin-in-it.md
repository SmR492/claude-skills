# Put a pin in it

Ref: `BL:put-a-pin-in-it` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 42. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-put-a-pin-in-it.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

"Put a pin in it" is an expression used in business and project management meaning to temporarily set aside a particular topic or issue to be discussed or addressed later, allowing the conversation or activity to move forward without getting bogged down. It avoids distraction and derailment when a topic is important but not immediately relevant. In project management it means recording an item or issue to be addressed later so nothing is overlooked or forgotten.
