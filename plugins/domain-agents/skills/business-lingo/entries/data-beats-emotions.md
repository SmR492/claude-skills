# Data beats emotions

Ref: `BL:data-beats-emotions` · Category: principle · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 89. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-data-beats-emotions.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

A quotation by Sean Rad, founder of Tinder, that data-driven decision making is superior to relying on emotions or gut feelings for important decisions; leaders and organizations should prioritize collecting, analyzing, and using data rather than relying solely on intuition or emotional reactions.

- Data provides an objective basis for decisions, helping identify patterns or trends, especially in complex or uncertain situations where emotions or biases may cloud judgment
- Data helps mitigate risk by enabling more accurate predictions and identifying potential risks or opportunities, allowing proactive rather than reactive steps
- Data-driven decisions are backed by evidence and can lead to better outcomes, higher efficiency, and improved performance
