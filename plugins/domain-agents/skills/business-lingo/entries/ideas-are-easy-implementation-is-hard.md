# Ideas are easy, implementation is hard

A quotation by Guy Kawasaki highlighting that coming up with an idea is the easy part while executing it is difficult. Often used in entrepreneurship, innovation and business, where ideas are plentiful but successful implementation is rare. Implementation requires careful planning, resource allocation, commitment, perseverance and attention to detail, plus a willingness to take risks by trying new approaches and learning from mistakes.

- Quotation by Guy Kawasaki
- Ideas are only the starting point; execution is the hard part
- Implementation requires planning, resources, a solid team and accountability
- Involves taking risks and learning from failure
- Emphasizes action and execution in achieving success
