# How to influence people

Ref: `BL:how-to-influence-people` · Category: technique · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 94. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-how-to-influence-people.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

Influencing people involves understanding human behavior, building relationships, and effectively communicating your ideas; it is about building positive relationships, understanding needs, and finding mutually beneficial outcomes, approaching each interaction with authenticity, respect, and a focus on shared goals.

- Build rapport through active listening, empathy, and credibility (expertise, integrity, reliability)
- Communicate effectively and clearly, avoid jargon, and align body language and tone with your message
- Find common ground and shared goals, highlighting mutual benefits
- Use persuasive techniques with logic, evidence, and stories or testimonials
- Be open to alternative perspectives, compromise when appropriate, and seek win-win outcomes
- Lead by example with integrity, respect, and professionalism
