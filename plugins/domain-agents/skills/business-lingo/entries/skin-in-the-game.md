# Skin in the game

Ref: `BL:skin-in-the-game` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 47. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-skin-in-the-game.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

"Skin in the game" refers to having a personal stake or investment in the outcome of a decision or venture, suggesting people act more in the project's best interest if they have something to lose. It was popularized by Nassim Nicholas Taleb, who argued many in power make decisions with little personal consequence while others bear the risks. It is applied in business and finance (stock options, performance bonuses), entrepreneurship, and even politics.

- Business/finance: personal financial stake via stock options and performance-based bonuses
- Entrepreneurship: founders invest their own money and time to align interests
- Politics: politicians affected by policies they enact may be more motivated for constituents
