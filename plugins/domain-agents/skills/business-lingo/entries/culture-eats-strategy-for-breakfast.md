# Culture eats strategy for breakfast

A famous quote attributed to management consultant Peter Drucker meaning that organizational culture is a more powerful force than strategy for achieving success. No matter how well-crafted a strategy is, it will not succeed unless supported by a strong, aligned organizational culture. Organizational culture (shared values, beliefs, attitudes and behaviors) affects engagement, productivity and talent retention, so organizations must ensure their culture is aligned with their strategy.

- Attributed to Peter Drucker
- Culture is a more powerful force than strategy for success
- Strategy fails without an aligned, strong organizational culture
- Organizations should align culture with strategy (innovation, purpose, transparency, accountability)
