# Big Hairy Audacious Goal (BHAG)

The term Big Hairy Audacious Goal (BHAG) was coined by James Collins and Jerry Porras in their book "Built to Last: Successful Habits of Visionary Companies". A BHAG is a long-term goal that is both ambitious and inspiring, challenging a company to think beyond its current capabilities, and should be a clear, compelling statement of the company's ultimate purpose or mission.

- Typically set for a period of 10 to 30 years
- Specific enough to be measurable, yet broad enough to inspire and motivate stakeholders (employees, customers, investors)
- Provides long-term direction and rallies employees around a common purpose
- Examples: Google (organize the world's information), Microsoft (a computer on every desk and in every home), Amazon (be the world's most customer-centric company)
- Should be realistic and achievable; a BHAG that is too unrealistic can be demotivating
