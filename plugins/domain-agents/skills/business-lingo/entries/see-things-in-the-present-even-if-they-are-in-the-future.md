# See things in the present, even if they are in the future

A quotation by Larry Ellison, co-founder of Oracle Corporation, implying the importance of visionary thinking and strategic planning. It reflects the belief that great leaders anticipate and shape the future by acting in the present, understanding current realities, trends and challenges while having the foresight to anticipate future changes and opportunities. It calls for a mindset unconstrained by current realities and for leaders to be proactive in shaping their future rather than reactive.

- Quotation by Larry Ellison (Oracle)
- Emphasizes visionary thinking and strategic planning
- Understand present realities while anticipating future changes
- Requires being innovative, open-minded and willing to take risks
- Be proactive in shaping the future rather than reactive
