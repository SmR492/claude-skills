# How to lead a meeting

Leading a meeting effectively involves careful planning, facilitation skills, and the ability to keep participants engaged and focused. Steps from preparation through closing.

- Define the purpose and objectives, and share an agenda with times in advance
- Prepare meeting materials, organized and accessible
- Start with an introduction, welcome, and overview of agenda and objectives
- Facilitate discussion, ensuring everyone can contribute
- Encourage collaboration where participants feel comfortable sharing
- Manage time: start and end on time, focus on the agenda, defer or delegate as needed
- Manage conflict calmly and respectfully, seeking common ground
- Close the meeting by summarizing outcomes and action items, and follow up with a summary
