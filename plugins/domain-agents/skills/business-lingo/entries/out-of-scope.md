# Out of scope

Ref: `BL:out-of-scope` · Category: term · Entry version: 1

"Out of scope" is a phrase used in project management, business analysis, and other fields to indicate that a task or activity is not within the bounds of the current project or assignment — not something one is currently responsible for or able to work on in the current context. For example, additional features a client requests that were not part of the original project scope are "out of scope" and require a new project or change request. It can also indicate that a problem or topic is not relevant to a particular discussion.
