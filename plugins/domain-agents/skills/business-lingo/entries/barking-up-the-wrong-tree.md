# Barking up the wrong tree

Ref: `BL:barking-up-the-wrong-tree` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 29. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-barking-up-the-wrong-tree.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

"Barking up the wrong tree" is an idiomatic expression conveying that someone is pursuing a mistaken or misguided course of action or making incorrect assumptions, with efforts or focus misdirected and unlikely to lead to the desired outcome. It originates from hunting dogs barking at the base of a tree, mistakenly believing prey was there when the actual target was elsewhere. Figuratively it cautions someone to reevaluate assumptions and redirect their attention.
