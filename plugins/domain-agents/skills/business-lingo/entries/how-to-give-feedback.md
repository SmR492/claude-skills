# How to give feedback

Giving feedback effectively is an important skill that contributes to personal and professional growth. Practices for delivering constructive, respectful feedback.

- Choose the right time and place for a private, uninterrupted conversation
- Use "I" statements to express your perspective and avoid being accusatory
- Be objective, focusing on facts rather than assumptions
- Be constructive with suggestions, examples, and actionable recommendations
- Balance feedback by starting with positives to set a supportive tone
- Be respectful, empathetic, and non-confrontational
- Encourage dialogue and active listening, giving the recipient a chance to respond
- Follow up and offer support to check progress
- Lead by example by being open to receiving feedback yourself
