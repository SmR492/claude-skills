# Five Whys analysis

Five Whys analysis is a problem-solving technique often used in manufacturing and engineering that involves asking "why" five times to identify the root cause of a problem. It drills down from the symptoms of a problem to its underlying causes, and can be used standalone or as part of a broader root cause analysis approach.

- A team starts with the symptom and asks why it is occurring
- Each answer is used to ask the next "why" question until the root cause is identified
- Should not stop at obvious answers; dig deeper to less obvious or hidden causes
- Solutions should address the underlying cause rather than just the symptoms, to prevent recurrence
