# In full swing

Ref: `BL:in-full-swing` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 36. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-in-full-swing.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

The idiom "in full swing" refers to a situation or activity happening or progressing at a high level of intensity, energy, or momentum, fully underway and operating at its peak or most active state. It implies something is fully developed, established, or at maximum productivity or engagement — at its busiest, most vibrant, or most effective stage.

- Events or Parties: lively, bustling, with everyone actively participating
- Sports or Games: players fully engaged and action at its peak
- Projects or Processes: progressing smoothly with all elements in place
- Seasons or Time Periods: at peak or most active state (e.g. "summer is in full swing")
