# Buy-in

Ref: `BL:buy-in` · Category: term · Entry version: 1
Source: business-lingo-guide Seite 32. Provenance: bulk extraction 2026-07-02, migrated to Orbit 2026-09-02 (riedl/nsai/doktrin/BL-buy-in.json); content unverified against a primary source. Treat figures and legal statements as unverified until checked.

"Buy-in" refers to the process of getting people to agree with and support a particular idea, project, or decision, which is crucial for success because it ensures everyone is on board and working toward the same goal. The term comes from the idea that people must invest something — financially or emotionally, such as time, resources, or political capital — to commit. Buy-in is not the same as agreement or compliance; it implies genuine belief in the value and importance of the project.

- Involve stakeholders in planning and decision-making so they feel invested in the outcome
- Clearly communicate the benefits and rationale so people understand why it matters
- Address concerns and objections and provide opportunities for feedback and input
